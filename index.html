Privacy notice. Lunan collects your name, contact details, home address, nationality, ID photo and number, passport details (foreign guests), signature, vehicle details, payment proof, and Senior/PWD ID details if you claim that discount. We use them to verify and process your booking, apply statutory discounts, keep the guest register required by local government and DOT rules, run self check-in, and keep security and tax records. Your IDs and proofs are stored securely, are never shared publicly, and are seen only by staff who need them. ID photos are deleted after the legal retention period (generally 1–2 years for tax and audit purposes). Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) you may ask to access, correct or delete your data by emailing lunanbyski@gmail.com, or complain to the National Privacy Commission.
